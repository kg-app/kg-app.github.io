How ValidAIt works

A falsificationist framework for stress-testing your business thesis — built to challenge claims, not confirm them.
1
Start an analysis
Name it, then add a document or your first claim. Documents are parsed automatically into a starting set of claims; you can also add claims by hand at any time.
2
Claims are challenged, not confirmed
Every claim gets an AI critique designed to find its weakest point. Attach evidence and the AI classifies it as supporting, contradicting, or merely qualifying the claim — status updates accordingly.
3
Tensions surface contradictions
When two claims conflict — like assuming both an EPCM and an Owner PM are your primary buyer — AI flags it as a tension that needs an explicit resolution.
4
Counterfactuals name the failure condition
For any claim, ask: what would have to be true for this to be false? AI proposes counterfactuals from its critiques; you can add your own and mark them confirmed or ruled out as you investigate.
5
Experiments turn analysis into action
Click "Generate experiments" and AI reviews your highest-priority tensions and counterfactuals to propose concrete tests — with the reasoning behind each one shown alongside it.
6
Everything is traceable
Click the 📎 icon on any item to see exactly where it came from — a document quote, an interview, or a manual entry — and its full edit history.
