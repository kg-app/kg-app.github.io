How ValidAIt works

A falsificationist framework for stress-testing your business thesis — built to challenge claims, not confirm them.
1
Start an analysis
Name it, then add a document or your first claim. Documents are parsed automatically into a starting set of claims; you can also add claims by hand at any time.
2
Claims are challenged, not confirmed
Select a claim in the table to edit it in the right panel. Add evidence and counterfactuals inline, then click Analyze to find its weakest point. The AI recommends a status you can apply or ignore.
3
Tensions surface contradictions
When two claims conflict, critique flags it as a tension. Open the Tensions workspace tab to review them, or use Analyze all to critique every claim at once.
4
Counterfactuals name the failure condition
For any claim, ask: what would have to be true for this to be false? Use the Counterfactuals tab to browse all counterfactuals and their linked claims, or add them inline on a claim.
5
Experiments turn analysis into action
Open Experiments from the toolbar and click Generate experiments. AI reviews your highest-priority tensions and counterfactuals to propose concrete tests.
6
Everything is traceable
Click the 📎 icon on a claim, linked evidence, linked counterfactual, or overlay row to open the provenance drawer with origin details and the full modification trail.
